# Privacy Policy — Hibrido Not Included

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how **Hibrido Not Included** collects, uses, and protects user information.

## 1. Data Collected
To function properly, **Hibrido Not Included** may collect and temporarily or permanently store the following information:
* **Discord IDs:** User ID, Server (Guild) ID, and Channel IDs.
* **Configuration Data:** Custom settings configured within servers for bot functionality.
* **Command Logs:** Usage logs for debugging and security purposes.

*Note: We do not store sensitive personal data or the content of private messages that are not directed as commands to the bot.*

## 2. Use of Data
Collected data is used exclusively to:
* Ensure the proper operation of the bot's features.
* Save your preferences and server settings.
* Identify bugs, errors, and improve application stability.

## 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. All data remains strictly within the bot's infrastructure and official Discord APIs.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion
* Data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the bot's services.
* If you wish to have your data or your server's data removed from our database, you may request deletion by contacting us.
